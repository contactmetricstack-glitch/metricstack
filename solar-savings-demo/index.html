Example assumptions (editable)

These are illustrative starting points for this demo. They are not Power Q's figures, not Hydro-Québec's official tariff, and not a quote. Changing the province loads a new example set; you can still edit every field.

Quebec defaults used here: about CAD 0.08/kWh, about 1,150 kWh/kW/year, CAD 2.80/W installed, 5.5 m²/kW, 2% annual price change, 0.5% degradation, and 0.0017 kg CO₂/kWh (a low example intensity, in the range often cited for a hydro-heavy grid). How the estimate is calculated

Annual electricity use is the monthly bill times 12, divided by the example rate. The suggested system in kW is the size you type, or else roof area divided by the example m² per kW (capped so it does not exceed about one year of use), or else the size that would produce about one year of use after direction and shading factors. Annual production is that size times the example specific yield, times the roof-direction factor, times the shading factor.

annual use = monthly bill × 12 ÷ rate
production = system kW × yield × direction factor × shading factor
year-1 savings = production × rate
installed cost = system kW × 1,000 × cost per watt
simple payback (years) = installed cost ÷ year-1 savings

25-year savings add up each year's production after the example panel degradation, valued at the example electricity rate after the example annual price change. CO₂ avoided is production times the example kg/kWh factor. Roof-direction factors range from 1.00 (south) to 0.55 (north). Shading factors range from 1.00 (none) to 0.55 (heavy).

Worked example

A CAD 150 monthly bill at the Quebec example rate of CAD 0.08/kWh implies 22,500 kWh a year. With a south-facing, unshaded roof and 1,150 kWh/kW/year, the bill-based system is 22,500 ÷ 1,150 ≈ 19.57 kW, year-1 production is 22,500 kWh, and year-1 savings are CAD 1,800. At CAD 2.80/W the example installed cost is about CAD 54,783, so simple payback is about 30.4 years. A 6 kW system entered by hand produces 6,900 kWh and saves CAD 552 in year 1, at an example cost of CAD 16,800; payback is the same because the rate, yield and cost per watt have not changed. These numbers will move as soon as you edit the assumptions, and they are not a Power Q quote.

Estimates only — not financial advice, not an energy audit, and not a Power Q proposal. Incentives, net metering, demand charges, snow, inverter losses and site conditions are left out unless you fold them into the editable assumptions.
