About MetricStack

MetricStack is a small set of free calculators from AHM Global, built for founders, indie developers, freelancers and online sellers who need a clear number before they commit cash. The tools are aimed at English-speaking markets, and they are written so a first-time user can follow the formula, not only the result.

AHM Global is based in Islamabad and builds practical commercial projects — from marketplace selling to simple software people can use without a login. MetricStack is the tools project: slow, useful pages that stay free, with enough explanation for a reader (and for search engines) to trust the maths.

What you will find here

The launch set is five calculators: LLM API cost, a SaaS metrics bundle, Etsy fees and profit, freelancer take-home pay after Upwork or Fiverr, and Amazon FBA / eBay seller fees. Each tool page includes how the calculation works, the formula, a worked example, and sources for the fee or list price we used, plus the date those figures were checked.

How we treat your data

Calculations run in your browser. We do not require an account, we do not use analytics or advertising scripts, and we do not store the numbers you type. See the privacy page for the short version.
