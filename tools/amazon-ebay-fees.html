How this calculator works

Amazon and eBay both take a share of a completed sale, but they do it in different shapes, which is why this page is a two-tab tool rather than one blended “marketplace fee.” On Amazon, a typical FBA sale pays a referral fee (a percentage of the total sales price, with a minimum) plus a fulfillment fee that depends on the item’s size tier, shipping weight and price band, plus — since 17 April 2026 in the US — a 3.5% fuel and logistics surcharge on that fulfillment fee. Storage, inbound placement, aged-inventory surcharges and advertising are extra and are left as optional fields so you can add the lines you actually incur.

On eBay, payment processing is bundled into the final value fee. For most US categories the published structure, checked on 14 November 2026 against eBay’s seller-fee pages, is 13.6% of the total amount of the sale up to $7,500 calculated per item, then 2.35% on the portion above $7,500, plus a per-order fee of $0.30 when the order is $10.00 or less and $0.40 when it is over $10.00. The “total amount of the sale” includes the item price, handling, shipping collected from the buyer (with some exceptions), sales tax, and other applicable charges. Some categories — books and media, jewelry, sneakers over $150, guitars, and others — use a different percentage; change the rate fields if your category is not “most categories.”

Profit on both tabs is what is left after marketplace fees, the cost of the goods, and (on eBay) the shipping cost you actually pay. Buyer-paid shipping is revenue; your postage or inbound freight is a cost. The result is an estimate for one unit, not an account-level invoice.

The formulas

Amazon referral = max(total sales price × referral %, minimum fee)
FBA fulfillment = rate-card amount × 1.035 (fuel surcharge)
Amazon profit = price + shipping charged − referral − FBA − storage − inbound − ads − COGS

eBay FVF = min(total, threshold) × 13.6% + max(total − threshold, 0) × 2.35% + per-order fee
eBay profit = item + shipping charged − FVF − insertion − ads − COGS − your shipping cost

The Amazon fulfillment lookup uses published US non-apparel, non-dangerous-goods rates for small standard (all three price bands) and for large standard in the $10–$50 band, including the peak card that runs from 15 October 2026 to 14 January 2027. Today, 14 November 2026, sits inside that peak window, so the peak box is ticked by default. If your item is under $10 or over $50 in the large-standard tier, or is apparel, hazardous, or bulky, type the fulfillment fee from Seller Central’s revenue calculator into the custom FBA field rather than trusting the mid-band lookup.

Worked example

Amazon FBA. A $24.99 home-and-kitchen item, no buyer shipping, 15% referral, large standard, 12 ounces, peak season, $8 cost of goods and no ads or storage. Referral is max($24.99 × 0.15, $0.30) = $3.75. The large-standard 8–12 oz peak rate for the $10–$50 band is $4.48, plus 3.5% surcharge ($0.16), so fulfillment is $4.64. Fees total $8.39. Profit is $24.99 − $8.00 − $8.39 = $8.60, a 34.4% margin on the selling price. Switch the peak box off and fulfillment falls to $4.20 × 1.035 = $4.35, and profit rises by about thirty cents — a reminder that the calendar matters as much as the size tier.

eBay. A $50 item with $10 buyer-paid shipping, no tax, most-categories 13.6%, $20 cost of goods and $8 of actual postage. The total amount of the sale is $60, which is over $10, so the per-order fee is $0.40. Final value fee is $60 × 0.136 + $0.40 = $8.56. Profit is $50 + $10 − $20 − $8 − $8.56 = $23.44. If the same order were at $9.00 all-in, the per-order fee would drop to $0.30, which is why the calculator switches that line automatically.

FAQ

What is eBay’s final value fee in the United States?

For most US categories, eBay charges 13.6% of the total amount of the sale up to $7,500 per item, then 2.35% on the portion above $7,500, plus $0.30 per order of $10 or less or $0.40 above $10. Some categories use a different percentage. There is no separate payment-processing fee on top of the final value fee.

How are Amazon FBA fees estimated here?

The calculator adds a category referral fee to a size-and-weight fulfillment lookup for US standard-size items, plus the 3.5% fuel and logistics surcharge in effect since 17 April 2026. Peak rates apply from 15 October 2026 to 14 January 2027. Low-inventory fees, storage utilisation, aged inventory, returns processing and inbound defects are not auto-calculated — add them in the optional fields or confirm a full preview in Seller Central.

When were these fees checked?

On 14 November 2026, against eBay’s seller fee pages and public write-ups of the US FBA 2026 rate card and Amazon’s 2026 referral and fulfillment update. Amazon’s own rate card in Seller Central is the authority if a cell disagrees with this page.

Is this financial advice?

No. It is a planning estimate for one unit in the US store. Other marketplaces, Professional versus Individual selling plans, store subscription discounts on eBay, and tax collection can all change the real settlement.
