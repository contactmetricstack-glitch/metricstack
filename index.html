Why free

No signup, no tracking, no catch on the maths.

These calculators are free because the useful work is the number itself. You should not have to create an account to check a fee or an API bill. Every calculation runs on your device. We do not set analytics cookies and we do not phone home with your inputs. If the site later adds reserved partner links or ads, that will be labelled — and the tools will stay free to use.

FAQ

Are these calculators free?

Yes. Every tool on MetricStack is free to use, with no account and no email gate. You can bookmark a page and come back whenever a rate changes.

How accurate is the LLM pricing data?

We check official provider pricing pages and show the date those prices were last verified. Figures are standard-tier list prices in US dollars per million tokens. Providers change rates, add peak-hour bands, and charge extra for long context, batch, or fast modes. Always confirm the live rate on the provider’s own pricing page before you budget.

Do I need to create an account?

No. Calculations run in your browser. We do not ask you to sign up, and we do not run tracking or advertising scripts.

Is this financial advice?

No. Results are estimates to help you think through a number before you list a product, accept a contract or ship a feature. They are not financial, tax or legal advice, and they are not a quote from the marketplace or API vendor.

Will this site show ads?

Not yet. The layout reserves a quiet partner and advertisement slot so the pages stay stable if ads or affiliate links are added later. There are no live ads or tracking pixels today.
